## DECISIONS (reversible)
- Default speed 230 wpm; personal speed half-life 14 days.
- "Evening" loan = 4 hours.
- Weekday deadlines roll forward ("by Thursday" on a Thursday = next Thursday).
- Engine lives in `lib/engine/`; app in root `app/`; `@/` path alias.
- Chapter text stored apart from chapter metadata (`chapter_texts`) so shelves show titles/word counts without a loan.
- Notes and highlights are private even from the owner; loans and sessions are visible to the owner (for the ledger).
- Works default to `visibility = 'owner'` and `is_placeholder = true`; Itu shares a work by switching to `members`.
- Loan slips are stamped in Africa/Johannesburg time.
- Expired loans cannot be renewed; they return to the shelf and the reader borrows again (bookmark carried).
- Until Supabase is connected, the loan desk works as a per-browser preview (cookie), clearly labelled.
- Highlight and note offsets are character offsets into the chapter text; bookmarks are word offsets into the whole work.
- Reader settings (theme, layout, size) are per-browser (localStorage), not per-account.
- Words count as read only once the reader has moved past a paragraph (or the chapter end is on screen) — conservative on purpose.
- Preview mode keeps up to 40 session summaries in a cookie; library mode keeps full sessions (last 90 days used for pace).
- The librarian assumes 30 minutes of reading a day until sessions show the reader's habit.
- The ledger shows only your own reading; an owner-wide ledger across readers is not built (would need Itu's call on privacy).
- Canon status is per work (canon / draft / apocrypha); per-chapter canon is not modelled.
- The front hall is now rendered per request (it reads the canon cookie).
- Writer's Room imports default to Draft + Only me.
- Pitch share links last 14 days by default (max 60); the sample excerpt is chosen by the author, never pulled from a manuscript automatically.
- Library-mode shelves sort by series, series order, then title; an unknown canon status is treated as Draft (never shown as canon by mistake).
- A stored pitch kit stays marked Placeholder until the author ticks "This kit is in my own words".
- The family wall and timeline open in the one-at-a-time form; JSON stays available for bulk edits.
- A new person/place/event gets an id from "new …"; Itu renames it if he wants a meaningful id (renaming updates references).
- Lineage and timeline are readable by any card holder (as before); editing is owner-only.
- The Keeper's proof copy is read-only and unrecorded, so it never skews readership counts or the Keeper's own pace.
- "Just returned" (C124) applies only to books the reader handed back, for one hour; loans that ran out keep their "back on the shelf when due" stamp.
- In preview mode the Writer's Room loan counts come from the visitor's own cookie desk (the only loans that exist there).
- "Just returned" everywhere (card, ledger history, work page, front hall, halls) uses one rule — `justReturned()`: handed back by the reader, within the last hour.
- "Fresh on the shelves" uses a work's `updated_at`, so sharing a work or editing its description also brings it forward (60-day window, 3 works).
- Setting a work aside also closes access for readers who have it on loan (their loan rows and notes are kept); restoring reopens it.
- A book counts as finished at 90% of its last chapter's words (readers skim endings and the tracker is conservative); rereads keep the first finish date.
- "Recently returned" shows one line per work (its latest return) and leaves out works out on loan again; 5 at most.
- On the last chapter the end card links to the ledger; in the Keeper's proof copy it links back to the Writer's Room instead (proofing isn't recorded).
- Search looks in chapter titles but never chapter text; chapter hits are listed only for words the work's own details don't match.
- Shelf order defaults to series order; "Recently changed" and "Title" are opt-in via `?sort=`.
- The full backup leaves out everything that belongs to readers (notes, highlights, loans, sessions) and pitch-link hashes.
- System serif font stack (no web-font download) to keep builds offline-safe.
- "Continue reading" shows only an open loan the reader has actually read in (books borrowed but never opened stay on the card page's "On loan now"); a chapter read through (90%) points to the next chapter.
- Preview mode keeps the last 20 loans (never trimming one still out) and 40 session summaries per browser, in a compact cookie shape (C48); library mode keeps everything.
- The borrow form's remembered period (C44) is per browser, like the reader settings.
- "Hold the covers still" is per browser (localStorage), like the reader settings; the device's reduced-motion setting still applies on its own.
- Notes downloads are named with the SA export date (C81).
- Loan reminders (C101–C121) all use one rule each: "due soon" = due back within 24 h (`dueBackSoon()`), "within the hour" = the last hour (reading room line, work-page slip), and the engine's own "Due back very soon" for the last 15 min; "no renewals left" = 3 renewals used. Wherever Renew can't work, the library says "return" instead.

- In Borrowing history the Finished stamp sits on the loan during which the finish happened (C97); a finish recorded outside any loan (e.g. the Keeper's proof copy, which isn't recorded anyway) stamps no row.

- A book counts as "read" on the halls by the same rule as the ledger's finished stamp (C13); the halls show the mark only to the reader who finished it.
